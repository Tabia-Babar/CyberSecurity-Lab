About

Cyber Crime Evidence Management System

Target Audience

Our main target audience is law enforcement agencies, cybercrime investigation departments, and digital forensic investigators.

Our Project

Our project, Cyber Crime Evidence Management System is designed to help manage cybercrime cases and maintain digital evidence records in an organized way.

What It Does

It allows authorized users to add, update, view and manage case information, making evidence management easier, faster and more organized.

Our Purpose

The main purpose is to reduce manual record keeping and improve the management of cybercrime investigations.
